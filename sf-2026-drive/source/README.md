# Source files

These are the inputs the Colab notebook reads.

- `precinct_results.csv` — one row per precinct. Report 9 of the Statement of the Vote, joined to the August 17, 2026 district crosswalk.
- `census_precinct.csv` — 2020 census counts and ACS 2020–2024 characteristics apportioned to those precincts.
- `precincts.geojson` — the 514 precinct shapes, WGS84.

The raw Statement of the Vote workbook and the census download are upstream of these three files. They are large and are not in this folder.
